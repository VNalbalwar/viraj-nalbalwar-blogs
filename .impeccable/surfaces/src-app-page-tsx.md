# Surface: Offcuts blog, home and post pages (Read, with a stop-motion motion layer)

Mode: Read. Visitor: a developer or recruiter arriving from search, dev.to or a link; they want to grasp one post fast, then read it comfortably.

Binding: nothing in the design language may come from the author's own life, projects, research or tools. The world is carved from outside him.

Unresolved: custom domain; search depth; whether Remotion video lands in the first release.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Offcuts is a cut-paper collage world. Every post is a small collage of scissor-cut shapes, and everything that moves does so in stop-motion steps, like animation shot on twos. It refuses the cream-paper serif blog, neon-on-black dev blog, and the grid of identical rounded cards.
OWN-WORLD: Paper-white (#FAF9F7) and black card stock (#141413) as the two themes; five saturated paper colours (persimmon #FF4F2E, sun #FFC21A, leaf #2BA36B, blush #FFB3C7, pine #0F4C44) used as shapes only; darker persimmon (#C9361A) for text accents. Bricolage Grotesque display with its width axis, Literata for reading, JetBrains Mono for code only. Shapes are straight-edged scissor facets with a soft paper shadow; everything else is flat, with hairline ink rules and no cards.
STORY: A reader meets a wordmark made of paper tiles that flip under the pointer, sees each post as its own generated collage, understands from a plain title and one-line description what it covers, and reads a calm 66ch column with figures, terminal replays and compare sliders that behave like paper layers.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim masthead (mini cut glyph, Offcuts, Posts, Tags, RSS, theme toggle). Left aligned, the word Offcuts as seven paper tiles at display scale idling in 8fps stepped jitter; one sentence of purpose below; the newest post beside its collage at roughly two-thirds width. No kicker, no stat row.
FORM: Cut-paper collage (Matisse-era paper cut-outs as a graphic tradition) with stepped, stop-motion movement; chosen by the author after the user rejected the offered catalog worlds and delegated the decision.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
