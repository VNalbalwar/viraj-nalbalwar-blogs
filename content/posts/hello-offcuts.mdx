I like it when systems misbehave. A cluster that slows down for no reason, a Linux box that was fine until it wasn't, a shared GPU where one workload quietly hogs the rest. I keep poking until I know why.

This blog is where that poking gets written down. I'm Viraj, and most of what I work on is Kubernetes, Linux and cloud infrastructure.

## What you will find here

Linux and systems first, then cloud and DevOps. Then the AI and ML news that is worth a second look, written from the original source and not from the headline.

## The rules I hold myself to

1. Every claim about the outside world links to where it came from.
2. If I did not run something, the post says so.
3. No quote goes in unless I can link the page it came from.

A post that breaks one of these gets fixed, not defended.
