This is a blog about things I try on real machines and what I find out. Some posts are experiments. Some are write-ups of a problem that cost me an evening. Some are news from AI and infrastructure, but only when I can point at the source.

## How a post gets made

A draft is researched and written with Claude, using web search for anything that happened recently. That draft arrives as a pull request. I read it, fix what is wrong or flat, check every link, and only then merge it. Merging is what publishes.

<Callout type="note" title="Why say this out loud">
Readers should know when a model helped write what they are reading. Saying so also keeps me honest about the review step.
</Callout>

## The rules posts follow

1. Every claim about the outside world links to where it came from.
2. If I did not run it, the post says so.
3. No quote goes in unless I can link the page it came from.

---

## What you will find here

Linux and systems come first, because that is where I spend most of my time. Cloud and DevOps next. Then AI and ML news, written from what I could reproduce, not from a press release.
